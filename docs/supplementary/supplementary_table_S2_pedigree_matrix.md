# Supplementary Table S2: Pedigree matrix assessment for emission factor parameters

## Overview

Uncertainty in emission factor parameters was characterised using the pedigree matrix approach (Weidema & Wesnæs, 1996), with uncertainty factors following Ciroth et al. (2016). Each parameter was scored on five data quality indicators, with scores converted to geometric standard deviation squared (GSD²) values for Monte Carlo simulation.

## Pedigree Matrix Scores

| Parameter | Value | Reliability | Completeness | Temporal | Geographical | Technological | GSD² |
|-----------|-------|-------------|--------------|----------|--------------|---------------|------|
| EF₁ (direct N₂O) | 0.005 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1.20 |
| EF₄ (volatilisation) | 0.014 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1.10 |
| EF₅ (leaching) | 0.011 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1.10 |
| Frac_GASF | 0.02 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 1.15 |
| Frac_LEACH | 0.10 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 1.25 |

## Score Definitions

### Reliability (data source quality)
1. Verified data based on measurements
2. Verified data partly based on assumptions
3. Non-verified data partly based on qualified estimates
4. Qualified estimate
5. Non-qualified estimate

### Completeness (data representativeness)
1. Representative data from sufficient sample of sites
2. Representative data from smaller number of sites
3. Representative data from adequate number of sites but shorter periods
4. Representative data but from smaller number of sites and shorter periods
5. Representativeness unknown

### Temporal correlation
1. Less than 3 years difference
2. Less than 6 years difference
3. Less than 10 years difference
4. Less than 15 years difference
5. Age unknown or more than 15 years difference

### Geographical correlation
1. Data from area under study
2. Average data from larger area in which study area is included
3. Data from area with similar production conditions
4. Data from area with slightly similar production conditions
5. Data from unknown or distinctly different area

### Technological correlation
1. Data from enterprises, processes and materials under study
2. Data from processes and materials under study but different enterprises
3. Data from processes and materials under study but different technology
4. Data on related processes or materials
5. Data on related processes on laboratory scale or different technology

## Justification for Scores

### EF₁ (direct N₂O emission factor)
- **Reliability (3)**: Based on literature values from rockwool and NFT systems, not direct coir measurements
- **Completeness (3)**: Limited number of soilless studies available
- **Temporal (2)**: Studies from 2017–2021
- **Geographical (3)**: European studies (Germany, Spain) applied to UK
- **Technological (4)**: Rockwool/NFT data applied to coir substrate

### EF₄ and EF₅ (indirect emission factors)
- **Reliability (2)**: IPCC 2019 Refinement values based on meta-analysis
- **Completeness (2)**: Global synthesis with regional disaggregation
- **Temporal (1)**: Recent IPCC update (2019)
- **Geographical (2)**: European/temperate values
- **Technological (2)**: Applicable to fertiliser N in general

### Frac_GASF (volatilisation fraction)
- **Reliability (2)**: UK national inventory values for CAN
- **Completeness (2)**: National average, not crop-specific
- **Temporal (1)**: Current UK inventory (2025)
- **Geographical (2)**: UK-specific but national average
- **Technological (3)**: Adjusted for drip fertigation based on expert judgement

### Frac_LEACH (leaching fraction)
- **Reliability (3)**: Based on semi-closed system characteristics, not direct measurement
- **Completeness (4)**: Limited data on UK commercial coir systems
- **Temporal (2)**: Recent literature on closed/semi-closed systems
- **Geographical (3)**: European data applied to UK
- **Technological (4)**: General soilless recirculation estimates
